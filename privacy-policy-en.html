03

How the website works

  • The contact and question forms prepare a draft in your email application. The website does not submit the form to a SMENA server. We receive your data only if you send the message yourself. If you choose Telegram, you paste and send the text yourself.
  • Service selection runs in your browser. Its result is added to the draft and reaches us only if you send it.
  • The website does not set cookies or load analytics, advertising pixels or third-party widgets. Language and theme are stored locally in your browser's localStorage and can be removed by clearing site data.
  • When you follow a link to an external service, its own privacy rules apply.

Your rights

You may ask what data is processed, request correction, blocking or deletion of inaccurate, excessive or unlawfully obtained data, and withdraw consent. Send requests to hello@smena.agency with your name, contact method and request. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity.

Withdrawal does not invalidate prior lawful processing or processing based on another lawful ground.

07

Security and deletion

We apply legal, organizational and technical safeguards appropriate to the nature, volume and circumstances of processing, including access controls and account protection. Data is deleted or anonymized when its purpose ends unless a contract or law requires continued retention.
